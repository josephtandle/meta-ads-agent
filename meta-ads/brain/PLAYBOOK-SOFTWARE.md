# Software Playbook

## How to use this file

Use this for apps, software, installed technology, and product-led services. Classify the buying motion before allocating spend. Read offer context and strategy first. Meta channel fit is a hypothesis to validate with qualified activation and revenue, not a conclusion from low CPM or sign-up volume.

## Prosumer versus enterprise: decide channel role first

Meta is strongest for prosumer, creator, owner-led, and individual-decision products. The buyer can understand value in a short demonstration, make the purchase or trial decision themselves, and act without a committee. In that context Meta can be an acquisition channel because creative can show the lived outcome and retargeting can close the understanding gap.

Classic enterprise is different. A committee, security review, procurement process, long sales cycle, and high ACV make intent capture, professional channels, outbound, and sales enablement more central. Meta is usually supplementary, often 8 to 25% of paid budget [practitioner-consensus], where it supports awareness, education, retargeting, or founder-led demand creation. Do not misdiagnose a static feature-list ad's failure with cold enterprise traffic as proof Meta cannot work. It may be a buyer-motion and creative mismatch.

## Funnel selection and structural conversion realities

Use trial or signup when a user can experience value quickly. Use a waitlist only as a pre-launch stopgap with an explicit nurture and response plan. Use a founder call, demo, or scoped consultation for high-ACV implementation and complex onboarding. Use content-led education where consideration is long and a demo cannot explain the category alone.

For apps, a hard paywall has converted about 10.7% of downloads to paid within 35 days versus 2.1% for freemium in one structural data set [benchmark-study]. The mechanism is filtering for intent, not a universal instruction to add a paywall. Free-to-paid can be 5 to 15% for strong products, with 2 to 5% more typical [benchmark-study]. Choose the model that matches time-to-value, trial costs, and retention. A hard paywall can clarify intent but may suppress learning, referrals, and activation.

Make the funnel decision explicit in the strategy. A product with immediate, legible value can use the ad and onboarding flow to earn a trial or purchase. A product whose value appears only after setup, team input, or migration needs an honest assessment, demo, or implementation path. Do not acquire a large volume of self-serve signups when the real product requires assisted discovery. That looks like efficient media until activation and retention arrive.

Instant forms can produce 30 to 50% cheaper leads but are noisier [practitioner-consensus]. For a software sales handoff, optimise for activated, product-qualified, or booked-demo outcomes rather than raw form submissions. CAC payback under eight months is a common operating target, with top performers nearer 2.5 to 5 months [practitioner-consensus]. Use these as a finance discussion starter, not as evidence that a product is ready to scale.

## Event laddering: preserve the scarce priority slots

Meta permits an 8-event cap per domain in the prioritised event configuration [meta-official]. Build an event ladder before campaigns start: page view, view content, lead or signup, trial-start, onboarding complete, activated, purchase or paid, and product-qualified/closed-won, adjusted to the actual product. Do not waste priority on redundant vanity events.

**Slot 1 iOS rule:** Slot 1 receives the strongest iOS-opted-out attribution treatment [meta-official]. Put purchase in Slot 1 if purchase volume can support it. If it cannot, put the shallowest affordable truthful event, often trial-start or completed signup, in Slot 1. CAPI should still send activation, paid, product-qualified, and closed-won events so optimisation can graduate down-funnel as volume grows.

The learning standard remains about 50 conversions per week [meta-official]. Use `target CPA x 50 / 7` to establish the daily budget needed for the selected event. If it is unaffordable, move one step shallower rather than holding an underfunded deep-event campaign indefinitely. Improve Event Match Quality with consented, hashed email and phone data where appropriate; an 8.8+ reference is a useful technical benchmark [practitioner-consensus], not a compliance exemption.

## Creative: specific outcome first, product second

**Never lead with AI.** “AI-powered” has become saturated category language: audiences see repetitive AI-UGC, vague promises, and identical claims across tools. It is now table stakes rather than a differentiated reason to care [practitioner-consensus]. Lead with a concrete, personally true outcome, operational pain, or revealed workflow. Explain AI only after the prospect understands what becomes easier or possible.

The founder-demo format is especially effective for an idiosyncratic product: first 2 to 3 seconds show the problem or desired result; then show a real screen, concrete workflow, and visible outcome; finish with one clear next step. Do not hide the product behind stock UGC or generic feature lists. A practical format ladder is 9 to 15 second problem-hook video for cold traffic, UGC transformation or explanation for mid-funnel, benefit-stack static for retargeting, and real urgency carousel for decision-stage traffic [practitioner-consensus].

Founder-demo work improves when it is treated as a reusable production system. Record one authentic workflow, then create distinct openings for different problem-aware segments, distinct proof moments, and distinct calls to action. Preserve the same product truth while changing the story. This produces useful contrast without turning the account into a collection of shallow “AI tool” ads.

Instrument two leading indicators. Hook rate is 3-second video views divided by impressions; a 30 to 40% baseline and 55%+ top-decile point are rough references [benchmark-study]. Hold rate is 15-second plays divided by 3-second views; 40 to 50% is a rough average, 60%+ strong, and under 30% indicates a pacing issue [benchmark-study]. Low hook means fix the first frame and proposition. Low hold after a good hook means reveal the proof or payoff earlier.

Creative fatigue moves quickly. One software-oriented data point places video fatigue around 9.2 days and static fatigue around 6.5 days, down from roughly 14 days in 2024 [single-source]. Treat those as monitoring windows, not expiry dates. Launch new concepts on a cadence and compare frequency, CTR, CVR, placement mix, and budget changes before calling an asset fatigued.

## Landing and interaction design

The landing page must continue the ad's promise in the first screen. Fix clarity before buying more traffic. SaaS median conversion is often cited around 3.8%, with the top 10 to 20% warmer [benchmark-study], but page performance is highly dependent on traffic temperature and offer. Keep social proof adjacent to the CTA, request three fields rather than nine when a form is necessary, and make the actual workflow observable.

An interactive demo is a credible way to reduce software uncertainty. Storylane and Factors report 24.35% conversion for engaged-demo visitors versus 3.05% baseline across 110K sessions [vendor-published]. That is vendor-published evidence, so treat it as an upper-bound hypothesis and measure it on the product's own traffic. A screen-recorded real workflow is the lower-complexity alternative.

## Speed-to-lead and signal feedback

When a signup, trial, waitlist, or demo requires human follow-up, operate a 5-minute speed-to-lead target. One cross-industry estimate says contact within five minutes can yield up to 9x the conversion of a 30-minute response [single-source]. The exact multiplier will vary; the operational lesson does not. Record time-to-first-response, attempt count, demo booked, activation, and sales outcome. Pass qualified outcomes back through CAPI.

## Cross-industry imports worth using

From ecommerce: creative velocity is structural. Top-quartile operators reportedly launch 22+ creatives weekly versus an 8-creative median [single-source]. Ten or more concepts per month is associated with roughly 31% lower CPA than fewer than five [benchmark-study]. A 12 to 18% win rate means volume is expected, not waste.

From games and apps: use hook and hold as early signals before conversion volume exists. Top game-UA teams may create 50 to 200 variants weekly [benchmark-study], a scale most software teams do not need, but the lesson is that a single “hero ad” cannot carry an account.

From local lead generation: return lead quality and activation quickly, concentrate budget rather than fragment it, and respond fast. From info products: real urgency works only when it is verifiable, such as a genuine dated price reversion or cohort deadline. Vague scarcity is not urgency and should never be invented.

## Measurement sanity check

Use platform results for delivery diagnostics and a measurement ladder for business decisions: Pixel plus CAPI with deduplication, product analytics, CRM, post-signup “how did you hear about us,” MER, and incrementality testing as spend warrants it. **MER sanity check:** divide total revenue by total marketing spend, including agencies, tools, creators, and media. A rough ecommerce reference of 3.0 to 5.0x MER [practitioner-consensus] is context, not a software target. The important question is whether blended efficiency, new-customer revenue, activation, retention, and payback support the channel.

If platform conversion is weak but blended revenue and survey evidence are improving, investigate assisted demand before cutting Meta. If platform conversion is strong but activated users and MER are weak, investigate attribution and product quality before scaling.

## A practical weekly review

Run account-level and campaign-level insights for the last 7 and 30 days. Join them to signup, activation, paid conversion, retention, and response-time data. Identify whether the constraint is signal, creative, landing clarity, onboarding, sales follow-up, or channel fit. Launch a small number of new concepts, preserve the control, and give each an explicit success metric and kill date. A software campaign should be allowed to fail honestly: if qualified activation stays weak after the basic system works, use the evidence to reallocate effort rather than invent a new audience theory.

## Operating rules

1. Treat a prosumer product as an install or activation funnel, not enterprise SaaS.
2. Lead with concrete outcomes and founder-demo specificity, never “AI-powered.”
3. Reserve the 8 event slots, obey the Slot 1 iOS rule, and optimise at the deepest affordable event.
4. Build interactive or observable product proof, then measure it against the vendor-published benchmark rather than assuming it transfers.
5. Respond in five minutes where a human handoff is promised.
6. Produce new concepts continuously, watch the 9.2/6.5-day fatigue windows, and refresh based on evidence.
7. Use real urgency only. If the deadline or price change is not true and documented, do not claim it.
8. If quality remains weak after signal, creative, page, and follow-up checks, pause or reduce Meta rather than forcing channel fit.

## Failure patterns to name early

**Cheap signup, weak activation:** the shallow event is producing action but not product value. Inspect onboarding, first-session clarity, audience promise, and activation instrumentation. Do not scale because cost per signup looks attractive.

**Strong demo engagement, weak paid conversion:** the demo may explain the feature but not a commercially important outcome, or pricing, implementation, security, and sales handoff may be unresolved. Compare engaged-demo cohorts with non-demo cohorts on activation and retention before deciding the demo is a win.

**High CTR, weak trial start:** the ad may be compelling but the landing page is vague, slow, or unexpectedly asks for effort. Match the first landing-page screen to the exact product moment shown in the founder demo.

**Strong platform ROAS, weak MER:** investigate attribution, existing-customer share, discounts, refunds, and retained revenue. The platform can be accurately reporting credited orders while the business is not gaining sufficient incremental contribution.
